Limitations:
- All generated traces are synthetic and deterministic; they are not
  representative measurements of realistic NoC workloads.
- The held-out set has only two families and eight traces, and is a deliberate
  distribution shift. These numbers do not establish generalization or SNN
  superiority.
- The rule, default SNN, and config 19 receive identical features/windows and
  labels. Family separation prevents near-identical generated variants from
  crossing the tuning/held-out boundary.
- Config 5 is a tuning-set selection only; no parameter was copied into
  production/default predictor RTL.
- No router integration, deadlock-safety proof, or hardware-cost measurement
  was performed.
